Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our scoring benchmarks, affiliate business model, and editorial ethics.

How does Detailed Review evaluate and score B2B SaaS and AI tools?

Every tool undergoes a minimum 14-day hands-on testing protocol in active production workflows. We audit 45+ empirical evaluation criteria weighted across 5 pillars: Feature Utility & Workflow Depth (25%), Output Quality & Latency Benchmarks (25%), Value for Money & True Seat Pricing (20%), Security & Compliance (15%), and Integration Ecosystem with API Reliability (15%).

How does Detailed Review monetize, and does it affect tool rankings?

Detailed Review is funded through affiliate referral commissions. When you purchase a software subscription via our links, we may receive a commission from the software provider at zero extra cost to you. However, vendors cannot buy high review scores, alter our testing verdicts, or pay for guaranteed top-3 placements. We routinely publish critical flaws and performance bottlenecks even for our top-ranked recommendations.

Are the pricing plans and promotional deals displayed accurate?

All pricing tiers, feature gates, and promotional offers are verified directly on vendor billing pages and updated on a recurring schedule with exact verification timestamps. Because B2B software vendors frequently modify usage caps and seat fees, we strongly advise confirming the final quote directly on the provider's checkout page.

Can software vendors sponsor a review or pay to be ranked #1?

No. We enforce a zero-tolerance policy against paid editorial placements. Our rankings are determined purely by our empirical scoring matrix and real-world workflow stress tests. We refuse sponsored reviews, and vendors have zero advance editorial veto over our published analyses.

How do you calculate the estimated ROI in your Interactive Matchmaker?

Our SaaS ROI Calculator combines average hourly engineering, marketing, and sales operational salaries ($45 to $75/hr depending on company tier) with empirical workflow acceleration rates observed during our 14-day automation benchmarks. The model accounts for onboarding overhead and tool license costs to present conservative, net-positive productivity projections.
