Affiliate Disclosure & Transparency Policy

In Accordance with FTC 16 CFR Part 255 · Last Updated: November 17, 2026

✓

The Short & Clear Summary

Detailed Review is funded through affiliate referral commissions. When you click on outbound links to software vendors on our site and make a purchase or subscribe to a paid plan, we may receive a commission from that vendor.

This commission comes at ZERO additional cost to you. In fact, our partnerships frequently unlock negotiated extended trials, coupon discounts, or exclusive promotional tiers.

1. FTC Compliance Declaration

In accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's (FTC) Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255), we disclose that Detailed Review maintains commercial affiliate marketing relationships with select software vendors, SaaS developers, and affiliate networks featured on detailed-review.com.

These relationships provide the financial resources required to maintain our independent testing laboratory, subscribe to high-tier commercial software plans, and fund intensive hands-on benchmark research without requiring a paid paywall for readers.

2. Our Non-Negotiable Editorial Independence Pledge

While we earn affiliate revenue, our testing methodology and editorial verdicts operate under strict separation from commercial interests:

  • Rankings Cannot Be Purchased: No vendor can buy a top-3 podium spot, increase their numerical score on our 0–10 matrix, or pay to replace an organic winner.
  • Flaws Are Always Published: If our empirical benchmark tests reveal that a software platform has slow API response times, unannounced billing traps, or frequent webhook dropouts, we document it prominently — even if the vendor offers a high affiliate payout.
  • Zero Pre-Publication Review Rights: Software companies have zero advance access or editorial veto over our published reviews, comparisons, or buyer sentiment analyses.
  • We Review Non-Affiliate Tools: Many platforms we evaluate and recommend do not have affiliate programs. If a non-affiliate tool provides superior utility for a specific workflow, it receives the score it mathematically earned.

4. Price Impact Guarantee

You will never pay more for any software tool by purchasing through our affiliate links. In fact, because we negotiate promotional arrangements with select vendors, clicking our affiliate links often unlocks exclusive signup bonuses, free credit tiers, or extended evaluation windows not available on standard vendor landing pages.

5. How to Identify Affiliate Links on Our Site

For total transparency, affiliate links on Detailed Review are tagged in the underlying code with rel="sponsored noopener" and typically lead to vendor checkout pages or official landing pages containing tracking parameters (e.g., ?ref=detailedreview).

Buttons labeled "Visit Official Website ↗", "Claim Free Trial", or "View Official Pricing" are typically outbound affiliate links.
