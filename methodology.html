The Engineering Behind Our Software Benchmarks

Standard Operating Procedure

Our 5-Stage Empirical Audit Protocol

Every candidate software undergoes this sequential 14-day protocol before any score or review is published.

01
Days 1 - 3

Provisioning, SSO & Onboarding Friction Audit

We sign up via paid corporate credit card accounts (never sponsored accounts). We configure Google Workspace / SAML SSO, invite team members, set up role-based access control (RBAC), and measure the total human minutes required to achieve an operational state. We test out-of-the-box templates versus custom configuration friction.

02
Days 4 - 7

Latency, Concurrency & API Reliability Testing

For workflow automation platforms (e.g. Make, Zapier), we trigger bursts of 10,000 webhook events to monitor rate-limiting behavior, queuing delays, and failure auto-recovery. For SaaS dashboards, we measure Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) and DOM rendering latency under heavy data table filtering.

03
Days 8 - 10

AI Output Quality, Hallucination & Consistency Stress Benchmarks

For AI platforms (copywriting, video generation, CRM intelligence), we run standardized 50-prompt test suites across complex domain topics. We evaluate factual grounding, adherence to custom brand voice rules, generation latency, and output consistency across repeated runs.

04
Days 11 - 12

True Seat Cost & Upgrade Traps Audit

We inspect the checkout terms, usage overage fees, API call costs, add-on costs, and forced annual contract lock-ins. We model out true cost curves for 1 founder, a 10-person team, and a 50-person enterprise to reveal the actual financial investment required.

05
Days 13 - 14

Security, SOC 2, GDPR & Sentiment Synthesis

We audit SOC 2 Type II audit report availability, GDPR data processing addendums (DPA), data residency guarantees, and AI model training opt-outs. We cross-reference our empirical findings against verified user reviews to identify recurring long-term technical flaws.

Pricing Accuracy & Currency Policy

Software pricing plans change rapidly. Detailed Review audits and updates pricing data on a recurring monthly schedule. All prices are listed in USD ($) unless otherwise stated. We always display the date on which a pricing plan was last verified.

Because software vendors frequently test dynamic pricing tiers, promotional lifetime deals, and localized pricing, users must always confirm final contract terms directly on the provider's checkout page before completing payment.
