Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Detailed Review ("we," "our," or "us"), accessible via detailed-review.com, operates an independent B2B SaaS and Artificial Intelligence software review portal. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding our policies on the collection, usage, processing, and disclosure of personal data when you visit our website.

We are committed to full compliance with global privacy regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU/UK) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

2. Information We Collect

Detailed Review does not require user account registration to read reviews, compare tools, or use our interactive SaaS ROI calculators. We collect minimal data categorized as follows:

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: When you contact our editorial board via email or submit a tool for review, we collect your name, corporate email address, software URL, and the contents of your message.
  • Automatically Collected Technical Data: When browsing our site, our hosting infrastructure and analytics frameworks log standard telemetry, including your Internet Protocol (IP) address (anonymized), browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, and timestamps.
  • Interactive Calculator Inputs: Data entered into our client-side SaaS ROI calculators (such as team size or primary bottleneck) is processed entirely within your local browser runtime and is never tied to your personal identity.

3. How We Use Cookies & Tracking Pixels

Cookies are small text files stored on your local device. We use cookies strictly for essential navigation, aggregated analytics, and affiliate tracking:

  • Essential & Functional Cookies: Necessary to maintain accessibility settings, dark mode preferences, and tab navigation state.
  • Analytics Cookies: Enabled via Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4 (GA4) with IP masking enabled to measure aggregate traffic patterns, dwell time, and site performance.
  • Outbound Affiliate Referral Cookies: When you click on an external link to a software vendor (e.g., Make, HubSpot, Apollo), a tracking cookie may be placed by the respective affiliate network (e.g., PartnerStack, Impact, ShareASale) to credit referral sales. These cookies do not store personally identifiable information and are governed by the third-party merchant's privacy policy.

4. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Compliance

We utilize Google Analytics 4 to understand reader navigation. Google Analytics collects anonymized statistical telemetry. We have configured Google Analytics with:

  • Automatic IP Address Anonymization;
  • Disabled Google Signals for personalized ad retargeting;
  • Restricted data retention period set to 14 months.

5. How We Use Your Information

Collected data is used solely to:

  • Deliver, operate, and optimize the Detailed Review website;
  • Respond to direct editorial inquiries, correction requests, and software submissions;
  • Prevent automated denial-of-service attacks, scraping abuse, and fraudulent activity;
  • Comply with applicable legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

6. Your Rights Under the GDPR (EU/UK Residents)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you possess the following statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You may request copies of personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request deletion of personal information where no overriding legal basis applies.
  • Right to Restrict Processing & Data Portability: You may object to or request transfer of your data in a structured machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@detailed-review.com.

7. Your Rights Under the CCPA / CPRA (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act:

  • We Do NOT Sell Your Personal Information: Detailed Review has never sold, rented, or monetized personal information to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers, and will never do so.
  • Right to Know & Delete: California consumers may request disclosures regarding personal information collected over the preceding 12 months, or request deletion of their records without discrimination.

8. Third-Party External Websites

Detailed Review contains hyperlinks to external third-party software websites, SaaS applications, and affiliate partner portals. We have no control over and assume no legal responsibility for the privacy policies, tracking mechanisms, or data practices of any third-party websites. We encourage readers to review the individual privacy policies of any external vendor visited.

9. Security & Data Retention

We implement industry-standard technical and operational safeguards, including HTTPS SSL/TLS encryption across all network transfers, to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We retain voluntary contact communications only for as long as necessary to fulfill editorial resolution.
