My Personal Code

My Product Testing Principles

Every score, critique, and recommendation published on Detailed Review adheres to these four non-negotiable rules.

01

If I wouldn't run my business on it, it doesn't get recommended.

High affiliate commissions never influence scores. If a tool has unreliable uptime, frequent webhook dropouts, or terrible support, I will not recommend it to readers. Period.

02

Output quality and latency over marketing copy.

Landing pages can promise the world. I test response latencies under heavy load, check token consumption costs, and stress-test hallucination rates with real edge-case data.

03

Exposing hidden seat costs and tier paywalls.

Software vendors frequently hide essential features behind $500/mo enterprise paywalls. I audit and document the real cost to achieve actual business utility.

04

Security, SOC 2, and data privacy are non-negotiable.

In an age of AI data scraping, I inspect model training opt-out clauses, GDPR policies, and SOC 2 Type II compliance to keep client data safe from leaks.
